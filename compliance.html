Local draft
Preliminary tool, not certification. No NCC 2025 numerical requirement is accepted as verified until the exact adopted source, edition, clause, applicability and evidence have been independently checked. Data stays in this browser unless exported. Do not enter sensitive client information on shared devices.

Project setup

Set the site and design classifications. The system activates the relevant inspection families and flags missing information.

Source controls

Site classifications are user-entered project facts, not inferred from the postcode. The project must confirm the edition and any Victorian amendments or transitional provisions before regulatory checks are released.

Finish-first product selections

Record selected systems before fixing supporting construction. Product dimensions shown in the catalogue are indicative options only and must be checked against the manufacturer's current technical data.

Supporting construction must follow the finish

For brick veneer, review cavity, footing support, frame location and weatherproofing. For bulk insulation, confirm installed thickness and whole-wall performance before determining stud depth. Other finishes trigger their own support, fixing and junction investigations.

Backward design readiness

Outstanding mandatory selections and source evidence prevent the design from being labelled ready for release.

Construction WBS and manual inspections

Applicable is the first column. Required items are activated automatically, while conditional tasks depend on recorded site conditions and building systems. Expand a row to inspect and record defects.
