STEP 2 OF 2

Select building finishes and products

Choose the finished materials first. The agent identifies the structural, insulation, support, fixing and weatherproofing details that must be resolved before design release.

How to complete this step

  1. Select the product category and system, such as brick masonry and brick veneer.
  2. Choose a proposed size or thickness. Confirm the exact manufacturer dimensions before approval.
  3. Enter the manufacturer, product name and installation manual link, if available.
  4. Use Add another product for roofing, windows, flooring, waterproofing or another wall elevation.

You do not need to know all products now. Missing selections will appear in Step 3, Design Readiness, and prevent design release.

How selections affect design

A brick veneer selection triggers checks on cavity, wall ties, footing support and the wall envelope. Bulk insulation requires verification of installed thickness and whole-wall thermal performance before the stud width can be finalised. Manufacturer details and NCC provisions still need source verification.

Backward design readiness

Outstanding mandatory selections and source evidence prevent the design from being labelled ready for release.
